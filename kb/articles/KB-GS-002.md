# Build your first workflow

_CloudFlow help center · Getting started · applies to all_

## Overview
This article explains how to add a trigger, add steps, and connect them in order in CloudFlow.

## Steps
1. Open **Workflows → New**.
2. Follow the on-screen prompts to add a trigger, add steps, and connect them in order.
3. Save your changes and verify the result.

## Notes
- Available on all. If you are on an older version, some menus may differ — see
  KB-ADV-VERSIONS-001.
