# Fix CF-422 validation errors

_CloudFlow help center · Troubleshooting · applies to all_

## Overview
This article explains how to correct the invalid step field shown in the error in CloudFlow.

## Steps
1. Open **Workflow editor**.
2. Follow the on-screen prompts to correct the invalid step field shown in the error.
3. Save your changes and verify the result.

## Notes
- Available on all. If you are on an older version, some menus may differ — see
  KB-ADV-VERSIONS-001.
