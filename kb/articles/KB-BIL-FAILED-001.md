# Failed payments and past_due status

_CloudFlow help center · Account & billing · applies to all_

## Overview
This article explains how to update your card to clear a failed charge and restore active status in CloudFlow.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Follow the on-screen prompts to update your card to clear a failed charge and restore active status.
3. Save your changes and verify the result.

## Notes
- Available on all. If you are on an older version, some menus may differ — see
  KB-ADV-VERSIONS-001.
