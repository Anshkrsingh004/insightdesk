# Upgrade or downgrade your plan

_CloudFlow help center · Account & billing · applies to all_

## Overview
This article explains how to change plans; limits apply immediately and billing is prorated in CloudFlow.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Follow the on-screen prompts to change plans; limits apply immediately and billing is prorated.
3. Save your changes and verify the result.

## Notes
- Available on all. If you are on an older version, some menus may differ — see
  KB-ADV-VERSIONS-001.
