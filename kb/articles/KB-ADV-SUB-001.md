# Sub-workflows

_CloudFlow help center · Advanced features · applies to 4.x_

## Overview
This article explains how to call one workflow from another and pass data in CloudFlow.

## Steps
1. Open **Workflow editor**.
2. Follow the on-screen prompts to call one workflow from another and pass data.
3. Save your changes and verify the result.

## Notes
- Available on 4.x. If you are on an older version, some menus may differ — see
  KB-ADV-VERSIONS-001.
